1/* Description: wheel velocity controls
2 *
3 * Instructions:
4 * 1) Initialize the wheels by calling wheels_Init() and encoder_Init()
5 * 2) Unbrake the wheels by calling wheels_Unbrake()
6 * 3) Set the wheel velocities by calling wheels_SetSpeed() and wheels_set_command_speed
7 * 4) Repeatedly call wheels_Update() every 10ms (as dictated by the variable TIME_DIFF) to ensure the wheels reach their speeds
8 * ==== instructions below not needed per se. Turning off the robot will do fine ====
9 * 5) Stop the robot by calling wheels_Stop()
10 * 6) Turn on the brakes by calling wheels_Brake()
11 * 7) Deinitialize the wheels by calling wheels_Deinitialize()
12 *
13 * Extra functions:
14 * 1) encoder_getCounter to get the last measured wheel speeds
15 * 2) wheels_DriverStatus returns whether board can communicate with the motor driver
16 * 3) wheels_DriverPresent returns whether motor driver has any errors
17 * 4) wheels_SetPWM set PWM of the motor, preferably use wheels_SetSpeed()
18 * 5) wheels_GetWheelsBraking returns whether the wheels are braking
19 *
20 * Notes:
21 *
22*/
