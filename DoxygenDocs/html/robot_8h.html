◆ Wireless_SendPacket()

void Wireless_SendPacket ( )

This function sends back a packet to the basestation.

During testing we found out that the payloadLength of the outgoing packet should be at least the payloadLength of the incoming packet. If this isn't the case we had trouble getting updated incoming packets. Thus REM_PACKET_SIZE_REM_ROBOT_FEEDBACK >= REM_PACKET_SIZE_REM_ROBOT_COMMAND At this point we have not figured out yet why this is the case.
